## Research

### Is Reasoning in LLMs and AGI an Illusion or Reality?
Published in IEEE.

Paper: https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11363969

This work examines whether current large language models demonstrate genuine reasoning capabilities or whether observed reasoning behavior is primarily emergent pattern recognition. The study evaluates reasoning claims in relation to broader discussions around artificial general intelligence.

### Current Research

**Machine Unlearning and Privacy in Machine Learning**

Investigating mechanisms that allow trained machine learning models to remove the influence of specific training data while maintaining model performance. The research focuses on:

- machine unlearning frameworks
- privacy leakage in trained models
- membership inference risks
- evaluation methodologies for privacy-preserving ML systems

## Selected Projects

### Personal Academic Assistant (RAG System)

Developed a question-answering system over personal academic notes using LangChain, OpenAI APIs, and ChromaDB. Implemented semantic chunking and metadata filtering for improved retrieval accuracy and integrated conversational memory to maintain contextual responses.

### GPT-2 Implementation from Scratch

Built an educational implementation of a 12-layer GPT-2 architecture with 768 dimensional embeddings, multi-head self-attention, positional encoding, and causal masking. Integrated pretrained weights and performed fine-tuning experiments to study training dynamics and output behavior.

### ArXiv cs.CL Topic Modeling and Clustering

Constructed a three-stage NLP pipeline to analyze over 44,000 abstracts from the ArXiv Computation and Language category (1991–2024). Generated transformer-based embeddings, applied UMAP for dimensionality reduction, and used HDBSCAN to identify latent topic clusters and analyze long-term research trends.
